Duckdoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Duckdoku mobile game (“Duckdoku”, “the app”, “we”) collects, why, and the choices you have. Duckdoku is a puzzle game made by an independent developer. We collect as little as we can: no account, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location are ever asked for.

1. Information stored only on your device

To save your game, the app stores the following on your phone. It never leaves your device:

  • Your level progress, seed balance, win-streak multiplier and saved power-ups
  • Your settings (music, sound effects, vibration) and whether you have seen the intro story

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your phone’s settings, deletes all of this.

2. The online leaderboard

The daily leaderboard shows players and the seeds they earned in the last 24 hours. To run it, the app signs you in anonymously (no email or password) and sends the following to our database, hosted by Supabase:

  • A random player ID created for your installation of the app
  • Your leaderboard nickname (a random name such as “Brave Duckling 42”, or one you choose)
  • The number of seeds earned or spent, and when

Your nickname and seed totals are visible to other players on the leaderboard. Please don’t use your real name or any personal information as your nickname. This data is used only to run the leaderboard; we don’t sell it or share it with anyone else.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Duckdoku is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner during play, a full-screen ad between levels, and optional reward videos you can choose to watch for in-game items. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This is done by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised, using Google’s consent tool. You can also reset or turn off personalised ads at any time in your phone’s settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. In-app purchases

Duckdoku does not sell anything for real money. In-game seeds can only be earned by playing or by watching optional videos.

5. Children

Duckdoku is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the leaderboard, contact us and we will delete their data.

6. How long we keep data

Leaderboard data (player ID, nickname and seed history) is kept for as long as it’s needed to run the leaderboard, or until you ask us to delete it. Data on your device stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

7. Deleting your data

To delete your leaderboard data, email us at YOUR_EMAIL@example.com with the subject “Delete my Duckdoku data” and the nickname shown in the game (on the leaderboard, “Playing as …”). We will delete your player ID, nickname and seed history within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone.

8. Security

All information sent by the app travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to the database is restricted so players can only change their own leaderboard entries.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
