🦊 Duckdoku 🦆

Help Fox find the hidden ducklings in cosy logic puzzles.

How it works
  • Place one duckling in every row, every column and every coloured pond.
  • Ducklings are shy: they never touch, not even diagonally.
  • Earn seeds, grow your win-streak multiplier and climb the daily leaderboard.
  • Bonus levels pay double seeds, but watch out for hidden bombs!
